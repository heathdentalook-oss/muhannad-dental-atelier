Dr. Muhannad, DMD

Fitted slowly, by hand, like a suit.

Dr. Muhannad earned his DMD at the University of British Columbia, then trained in aesthetic and implant dentistry, learning porcelain layering at the bench beside master ceramists.

He opened the Atelier to work the way those labs do. One patient in the studio at a time, a ceramist at every try-in, and no smile bonded before you have worn its trial version home.
